Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 17, 2026

Margdarshak Shikshan Sansthan ("Margdarshak", "we", "us", or "our") operates a WhatsApp-based messaging platform that enables businesses to communicate with their customers through the WhatsApp Business Platform (Cloud API). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your information when you use our services.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information:

  • Account Information: Your name, business name, phone number, and email address when you sign up for our platform.
  • WhatsApp Business Data: WhatsApp Business Account (WABA) details, phone number IDs, and message template information when you connect your WhatsApp account through our platform.
  • Message Data: Content of messages sent and received through our platform, including text, media, and delivery status.
  • Contact Information: Phone numbers and names of contacts you communicate with through our services.
  • Usage Data: Information about how you interact with our platform, including login times, features used, and message volumes.
  • Technical Data: Browser type, IP address, device information, and operating system collected automatically when you access our platform.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

  • To provide and maintain our WhatsApp messaging services.
  • To send and receive messages on your behalf through the WhatsApp Business Platform.
  • To manage your WhatsApp Business Account, message templates, and campaigns.
  • To provide customer support and respond to your inquiries.
  • To monitor and improve the performance and reliability of our platform.
  • To comply with legal obligations and Meta's platform policies.
  • To send you service-related notifications and updates.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share your data only in the following circumstances:

  • Meta / WhatsApp: Message data is transmitted through Meta's WhatsApp Business Platform (Cloud API) as required to deliver the messaging service. Meta's own data practices are governed by their privacy policy.
  • Service Providers: We may use third-party services for hosting, analytics, or infrastructure that process data on our behalf under strict confidentiality agreements.
  • Legal Requirements: We may disclose information if required by law, court order, or government regulation.

4. Data Storage and Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. These measures include encrypted data transmission, secure server infrastructure, and access controls. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

5. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide our services. Message data is retained for a reasonable period necessary for service delivery and compliance. When you request account or data deletion, we will remove your data within 30 days, except where retention is required by law.

6. Your Rights and Data Deletion

You have the following rights regarding your data:

  • Access: You can request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correction: You can request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Deletion: You can request deletion of your personal data at any time.
  • Portability: You can request your data in a portable format.
  • Withdrawal of Consent: You can withdraw consent for data processing at any time.

To exercise any of these rights, including requesting deletion of your data, please contact us using the details provided below. We will respond to your request within 30 days.

7. Third-Party Links and Services

Our platform may contain links to third-party websites or services. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these external sites. We encourage you to review their privacy policies before providing any personal information.

8. Children's Privacy

Our services are not intended for individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we become aware that we have collected data from a minor, we will take steps to delete it promptly.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically. Continued use of our services after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
